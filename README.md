### 🚀 About Me

I’m a data scientist with a Master’s from FSEGT in Tunis, Recently completed my final-year project at ARTES, where I built a demand forecasting system for the Tunisian car market. It combines SARIMAX, Prophet and XGBoost on more than 445,000 registration records and reaches around 10–13% MAPE, with the results shown in a Streamlit dashboard. I enjoy turning messy data into clear, usable insights, and I’m open to opportunities in data science and business intelligence.
